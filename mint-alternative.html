Mint alternative

Still missing Mint? Here's a budget app that does the tracking for you.

Intuit shut Mint down in 2024 and pointed its users to Credit Karma, which isn't a budgeting app in the same way. If what you liked about Mint was connecting your accounts and seeing where the money went without much effort, Money Laundering does that, and then has opinions about it.

What Mint users will recognise

  • Automatic bank and card sync through Plaid (US banks), so charges arrive on their own.
  • Spending by category and a monthly picture of where it all went.
  • Bills and subscriptions found from your history, with reminders.
  • Goals for saving up or paying off a card by a date.

What's new compared with Mint

  • One daily number: what you can spend today after bills and savings.
  • A cash flow calendar with your balance on every day of the month, before it happens.
  • Swipe to judge: every charge gets a verdict, clean or dirty.
  • A monthly report card, graded.
  • A cat, a town and games that make opening a budget app something you actually do. Turn them off with "Just the budget" if you don't want them.

Price

Free if you type transactions in yourself. Bank connections are $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year, US only. There are no ads, and we don't sell your data or show you credit card offers.
