Money Laundering, in five minutes.

In one paragraph

Money Laundering is a budgeting app for iPhone that gives you one number to live by each day, asks you to judge every charge clean or dirty with a swipe, and roasts your spending at the level you pick. Underneath it's a serious budget: a cash flow calendar, bill and subscription tracking, savings goals and a shared plan for couples. On top, a cat named Mouse moves from an alley to a penthouse as you build better habits, and an optional town called Lint Lane has fishing, a garden, a pub with pool and arcade games, and friends who can visit, all playable in iMessage too. The name is the joke: it's the legal kind of money laundering.
