YNAB alternative

A YNAB alternative for people who'd rather swipe than balance categories.

YNAB is a good app with a well-known method: give every dollar a job, category by category. If that works for you, keep it. If you've tried it and found yourself spending Sunday nights moving money between categories, Money Laundering takes a different route.

The short version

YNAB asks you to plan every dollar ahead. Money Laundering works out one number, what you can spend today after bills and savings, and asks you to judge each charge after it happens: clean or dirty, one swipe.

Who it's for

People who want to know, at a glance, whether today's coffee is fine. People who budget better when it's a little bit of a game. Couples who want one plan on two phones. If you need Android or a desktop app, YNAB is the better pick today.
