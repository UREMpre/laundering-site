Budget app privacy

Which budget apps use your data for advertising?

Your budget app sees every purchase you make. Apple requires every app to publish a privacy label saying what data it collects and what it's used for, including advertising and tracking. We read the labels of the best-known budgeting apps on the US App Store on November 26, 2026. Here is what they say, word for word from each label.

The short version

  • Of the nine, Rocket Money is the only one whose label lists Third-Party Advertising: Purchase History and Payment Info, linked to your identity.
  • EveryDollar and Quicken list Data Used to Track You, which means data linked with other companies' data for advertising or shared with data brokers.
  • Seven of the nine list data used for the developer's own advertising or marketing. Rocket Money's includes credit and other financial info, your name and physical address.
  • Copilot and Money Laundering list none of the three.

"Third-party advertising" and "Developer's advertising or marketing" are purposes listed under "Data Linked to You". Each app name links to its App Store page, where the full label is under App Privacy → See Details.

What these terms mean

Data Used to Track You: Apple's definition of tracking is linking data from the app with data from other companies' apps, websites or offline sources for targeted advertising or measuring ads, or sharing it with data brokers.

Third-Party Advertising: showing other companies' ads in the app, or sharing data with companies that show ads.

Developer's Advertising or Marketing: the app maker's own ads and marketing, such as promoting its other products or sending marketing messages.

Linked to You: kept in a way that's tied to you, for example to your account, device or name.

A closer look at Rocket Money

Rocket Money's label lists data used for Third-Party Advertising, linked to your identity: Purchase History and Payment Info. Under Developer's Advertising or Marketing it lists Purchase History; Credit Info and Other Financial Info; Physical Address and Name; Other User Content; User ID and Device ID; and Product Interaction and Advertising Data. It also lists data used for analytics and for product personalization, including financial info. The label does not list any "Data Used to Track You".

What ours says, and why

Money Laundering's label lists every data type as used for one purpose, making the app work, and nothing for advertising, marketing, analytics about you or tracking. It's not empty, and here's why each line is there: Purchase History is your subscription. Other Financial Info is the encrypted bank connection we hold if you subscribe. Contacts, Other User Content and Gameplay Content come from the optional friends feed and games. User ID is your Sign in with Apple account. Your transactions and balances stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Read how that works.

Check any app yourself

On the App Store, open the app's page, scroll to App Privacy and tap See Details. Look for "Data Used to Track You" and "Third-Party Advertising". Labels change when apps update, so check before you connect your bank.
