Private by design

Your money is your business. We built the app so it stays that way.

A budget app sees the most private numbers in your life. Most of what Money Laundering does happens on your iPhone, and what does leave it is kept to the minimum the feature needs. Here is how it works, in plain words. The privacy policy has the full detail.

The short version

  • Your transactions and balances live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, not on our servers.
  • No ads, no ad networks, and no analytics or tracking software from other companies in the app.
  • We never sell your data, and we never use your financial information for advertising.
  • Our App Store privacy label lists every data type as used for app functionality only, with nothing used to track you.

Where your money data lives

Typing it in by hand (free): what you type stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud backup. We receive nothing about you or your money.

Your own bank keys (free): the app talks to the bank connection service (Plaid) straight from your iPhone. Your bank data never passes through us.

Bank sync through us (subscription): your iPhone asks our server, our server asks Plaid, and the answer goes straight back to your iPhone. We don't store your transactions or balances, and we don't read them. The bank connection tokens we need are stored encrypted. You sign in to your bank on Plaid's screen, so we never see your bank password.

Sharing a budget with your partner

A household shares one plan on two phones. Here is exactly what happens when you invite someone:

  • AirDrop: your setup goes straight from your iPhone to theirs. Nothing passes through us.
  • A link: your iPhone locks your setup with a new key before it leaves the phone. The key travels only inside the link, in the part after the # that browsers never send to a server. We hold the locked copy, which we can't read, for at most 15 minutes, or until it's first opened, then delete it. We keep no record of what was in it.
  • After you've joined: the household's plan, sorting and goals sync between your two iCloud accounts through Apple's CloudKit, not through our servers.

The fun parts are opt-in

Friends, the friends feed, public profiles and games with friends are off until you turn them on and pick a username. Friends never see an amount of money unless you choose to show amounts. The town, the fishing and the cat run on your iPhone, and the backup of them is in your iCloud.

What we do keep, and why

Some features can't work without a small record on our server. We keep it to what each one needs:

  • Sign in with Apple: an account identifier, only if you subscribe or use a feature that needs an account, like friends, referrals or games with friends. Your email only if you choose to share it.
  • Subscription status from Apple, so we know it's active. We never see your card.
  • Friends and posts, if you turn the friends feed on.
  • An anonymous install count: a random number not tied to you, so we know how many copies are in use.

You can delete your account in the app's Settings, and "Erase everything" deletes your iCloud data too.
