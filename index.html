Questions

Is this… allowed?

Does it actually launder money?

No. It's a budgeting app. It doesn't move, hold or transfer money, and it has no payment features. The only thing it cleans up is your spending.

Is it a good YNAB or Mint alternative?

If you want a budget app for iPhone that tells you what you can spend today, tracks bills and subscriptions, forecasts your cash flow and makes budgeting something you actually open, yes. It's iPhone-only (with Apple Watch and iMessage), and bank sync is US-only for now.

Is my bank login safe?

Bank connections go through Plaid, and you sign in to your bank on Plaid's screen: we never see your bank password. Read the privacy policy for exactly what is kept and where.

Do I have to play the games?

No. Turn on "Just the budget" and the town, the cat's house and the games disappear. The budget works the same.

What are Suds?

The town's play coins. You earn them by using the app, sorting charges and having good days. They can't be bought, sold or cashed out.

Is the roasting mean?

Pick your level: Chill, Default or Unhinged. Unhinged swears and asks before you turn it on. Nothing shared with friends ever swears.
