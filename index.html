Money Laundering

A budgeting app for iPhone. It gives you one number to live by each day, sorts every charge into "needed" or "laundered", and tells you what it thinks of your spending.

  • One daily number. Income and bills spread across the month, so you know what today can cost.
  • Swipe to sort. Every charge gets a verdict. The washer keeps score.
  • Built for two. A household shares one picture of the money.
