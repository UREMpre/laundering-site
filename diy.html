Link your banks yourself

Money Laundering · the free, do-it-yourself way · Updated November 11, 2026
The short version
  • Money Laundering can link your banks for free if you bring your own keys from Plaid, the service banks use to share data with apps.
  • It takes about fifteen minutes, once. Your keys and your bank data stay on your iPhone; nothing passes through us.
  • Prefer not to? The subscription does all of this for you.

1. Make a free Plaid account

Go to dashboard.plaid.com/signup and sign up. Plaid will ask what you are building: a personal budgeting tool for your own accounts is a fine answer.

2. Ask for real bank data

A new Plaid account starts in a practice mode with fake banks. In the Plaid Dashboard, request access to real data (Plaid calls this Production; a free Trial plan covers up to 10 bank logins). Plaid asks a few questions about how the data is used and kept. Approval can take from minutes to a few days, and some banks (American Express, Chase and other banks that sign you in on their own site) need an extra approval that Plaid handles from the same page.

3. Add the app's return address

Some banks send you to their own site to sign in and then back to the app. For that to work, add this address in the Plaid Dashboard under Developers → API → Allowed redirect URIs:

https://ledger.615.media/plaid/oauth

4. Copy your two keys

In the Plaid Dashboard, open Developers → Keys. You need two things: the client_id and the Production secret. Treat the secret like a password: anyone who has it can read the banks linked to your Plaid account.

5. Enter them in the app

In Money Laundering, choose Use your own keys on the first screen (or Settings → Your own keys later). Paste the client_id and the secret, leave the environment on Production, and tap Check & save. The app checks the keys with Plaid and stores them in the iPhone's Keychain.

6. Link a bank

Settings → Link a bank or card. Pick your bank and sign in on Plaid's screen. Your history arrives over the next few minutes.

Good to know

  • On the free Trial plan each new bank login uses one of 10 slots, and removing a bank does not give the slot back. Adding more accounts from a bank you already linked does not use a slot.
  • To put the app on a partner's iPhone with the same setup, use Settings → Share setup. That link carries your Plaid secret, so show it as a QR code or AirDrop it; do not post it anywhere.
  • If you change the secret in the Plaid Dashboard, enter the new one in Settings → Your own keys.
  • Plaid's own help is at plaid.com/docs. We cannot see or reset anything in your Plaid account.
